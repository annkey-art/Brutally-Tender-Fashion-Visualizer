Fashion Visualizer

Discover your favourite cut before deciding on colours, prints and shopping.

1. Choose your size

Your size is the starting point. This demo compares styles; real shop availability and each brand's measurements are not yet connected.

Please choose your size to begin.

2. Add your photo

Use a front-facing, full-length photo. Your photo remains on your device. No account or upload to our server is required.

Your outfit preview will appear here.
Choose your size and a photo, then try a garment.

Adjust selected garment

Drag a garment on the photo, or use the buttons.

3. Try different cuts

Select a category and tap an item to see the shape on your photo. Combine a skirt and jacket, for example.

Coming next: 3–5 real shopping links filtered by size and stock availability. This version is a style preview only, not an online shop.

This visualizer can be personalized later for menswear, accessories, colours, patterns and individual store catalogues.

4. Build your look

You can combine garments, then save the result as an image. Different outfits can be compared by changing one cut at a time.

Garments are manually movable PNG overlays. The preview is approximate, not a measurement or guarantee of fit. Your EU size will become a filter for real products once a retailer catalogue is connected.
